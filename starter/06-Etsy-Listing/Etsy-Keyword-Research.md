## Keywords to Avoid

### “Make money fast”

Reason: Can look spammy and create unrealistic expectations.

### “Guaranteed sales”

Reason: Risky claim; avoid promises Etsy buyers may dispute.

### “OnlyFans AI model”

Reason: Different market, higher policy risk, may attract wrong buyer.

### “Deepfake”

Reason: Negative trust signal and potential policy issues.

### “Celebrity AI”

Reason: Trademark/personality-rights risk.

## Keyword Gaps / Positioning Opportunities

### 1. AI influencer beginner kit

Most prompt products sell prompts only. This product can position as a beginner system: workbook + prompts + captions + hooks.

### 2. Face consistency prompts

Many buyers struggle with keeping the same AI face. Make this a visible feature in images and description.

### 3. Faceless creator brand

A broader audience wants to build content brands without showing their own face. Use this in secondary copy.

### 4. Rio / travel influencer angle

Travel AI influencer prompts can create a premium visual angle. Useful later for Pinterest and examples.
