HOW TO USE THIS TOOLKIT

Recommended production path:

1. Open the workbook first.
2. Choose your niche, audience, and creator persona.
3. Read the Production Workflow (master system).
4. Build 20–30 ideas from the Content Ideas bank / worksheet.
5. Fill the 7-Day Launch Calendar, then the 30-Day calendar.
6. Pick prompts from the PDF bundle (or full prompts folder).
7. Generate images with your preferred AI image tool.
8. Run a quick quality check (face, hands, lighting, voice).
9. Polish in Canva using the size specs and template briefs.
10. Publish on Instagram first; repurpose later to X / Shorts / TikTok.
11. Batch weekly; review performance; refresh ideas monthly.
