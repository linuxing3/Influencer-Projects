No. This is a digital download. No physical product will be shipped.

No. The toolkit is beginner friendly and includes copy-paste prompts and templates.

### Can I use this for Instagram, X, YouTube, and TikTok?

Yes. The bundle includes prompts, X templates, YouTube hooks, and Instagram captions that can be adapted across platforms.

### Is this only for AI influencers?

No. The product is now positioned for broader content creators. It includes AI influencer and virtual creator prompts, but the main focus is content creation and creator growth.

### Does this guarantee followers or income?

No. It provides a content creation system and templates, not guaranteed results.
