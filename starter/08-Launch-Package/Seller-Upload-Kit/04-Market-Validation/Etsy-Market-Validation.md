Because the inspector strategy is commercially sound and “AI Influencer” is likely an emerging/low-search phrase, reposition the product away from narrow `AI Influencer Starter Kit` wording and toward broader buyer-intent terms:

- Primary packaging: **AI Content Creator Toolkit**
- Alternate positioning: **AI Creator Growth Bundle**
- Secondary angle: **Faceless Creator Business Kit**

Use “AI influencer” inside the product as a feature/bonus, not as the main search phrase.
