# Rebrand Decision — From AI Influencer to AI Content Creator

## Decision

Rename the public Etsy-facing product from:

```text
AI Virtual Influencer Starter Kit
```

to:

```text
AI Content Creator Toolkit
```

## Alternate Name for Graphics

```text
AI Creator Kit
```

## Premium Bundle Name

```text
AI Creator Growth Bundle
```

## Why

The narrow phrase “AI influencer” is interesting but likely too early and too low-search for a first Etsy listing. Buyers are more likely to search broader, established phrases:

- content creator
- social media kit
- Instagram templates
- creator planner
- YouTube growth
- faceless content
- AI prompts

The product still includes AI influencer prompts, virtual influencer guidance, and face consistency prompts, but those become features inside a broader creator-growth product.

## New Positioning

**For creators, freelancers, coaches, influencers, and AI users who struggle to create content consistently.**

This bundle helps them launch a content brand faster with:

- 100 AI Prompts
- 50 X Templates
- 30 YouTube Hooks
- 30 Instagram Captions
- Workbook

Launch Your Content Brand Faster
