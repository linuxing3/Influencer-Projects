Instagram Size Specs

Pixel Sizes for Feed, Reels, Stories, and Profile

Sizes change occasionally. If something looks cropped in-app, prefer 4:5 feed and 9:16 vertical as safe defaults.

Core Sizes

Use Aspect Pixels (W×H) Notes
Feed portrait (recommended) 4:5 1080 × 1350 Best grid presence
Feed square 1:1 1080 × 1080 Classic; less vertical impact
Feed landscape 1.91:1 1080 × 566 Rare for influencers
Carousel slide 4:5 1080 × 1350 Keep all slides same size
Story 9:16 1080 × 1920 Safe zone: keep text inward
Reel / Reels cover 9:16 1080 × 1920 Cover = first impression
Profile photo 1:1 320 × 320 min (export 800+) Clear face crop
Highlight cover 1:1 1080 × 1080 art, circular crop Consistent icons

Safe Zones

Stories & Reels (9:16)

Keep critical text and face away from:

  • Top ~250 px (username / audio)
  • Bottom ~350 px (caption / buttons)
  • Side edges ~50–80 px

Feed

  • Avoid tiny details at extreme edges
  • Faces slightly above center work well on mobile
