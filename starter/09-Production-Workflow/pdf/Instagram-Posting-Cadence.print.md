# Instagram Posting Cadence

## Guidelines for Feed, Reels, and Stories

These are **defaults for beginners**, not laws. Consistency beats the “perfect” time slot.

<div class="pagebreak"></div>

# Recommended Frequencies

## Launch (first 7–14 days)

| Surface | Frequency |
|---------|-----------|
| Feed or carousel | 3–5 per week |
| Reels | 3–5 per week |
| Stories | 4–7 days per week (1–5 frames) |
| Total public posts | ~1/day average |

## Steady state (after week 2)

| Surface | Frequency |
|---------|-----------|
| Feed / carousel | 2–4 per week |
| Reels | 3–5 per week |
| Stories | Most days |
| Soft promo | ≤ 20% of feed/Reels |

## Minimum viable (busy weeks)

- 3 Reels **or** 3 feed posts
- Stories on 3 days
- One weekly batch session still happens

<div class="pagebreak"></div>

# Format Mix

Aim for discovery + identity:

| Goal | Lean on |
|------|---------|
| Growth / reach | Reels |
| Brand grid / aesthetic | Feed 4:5 photos |
| Authority / saves | Carousels |
| Daily presence | Stories |
| Conversion | Stories + link in bio after value posts |

**Simple weekly recipe (example)**

- 2 photo feed posts
- 1 carousel
- 3 Reels
- Stories on 5 days
- 1 soft promo somewhere in the mix

<div class="pagebreak"></div>

# Timing Guidelines

Platform algorithms change. Use these as starting points, then trust **your** insights after 2–4 weeks.

| Window | Why people try it |
|--------|-------------------|
| Weekday morning (local) | Commute / coffee scroll |
| Lunch | Midday break |
| Early evening | After work peak |

**Rules that matter more than the clock:**

1. Post when you can engage for 10–15 minutes after.
2. Do not post five times in one hour then vanish for a week.
3. Keep a steady weekly rhythm.

<div class="pagebreak"></div>

# Grid Rhythm

- Alternate calm portraits with busier scenes
- Avoid three promo posts in a row
- Keep colors loosely related to brand palette
- Profile should “read” niche in 3 seconds

<div class="pagebreak"></div>

# Stories Cadence

| Day type | Story ideas |
|----------|-------------|
| Publish day | Behind the post, poll, “new post” |
| Off day | Mood, tip sticker, Q&A, poll |
| Promo day | Soft product frame + disclosure if needed |

Keep Stories casual. They support the brand without needing perfect AI stills every time.

# Secondary Platforms

Only after Instagram feels stable for 2+ weeks:

| Platform | Cadence suggestion |
|----------|--------------------|
| X | 3–7 short posts/week (repurposed hooks) |
| TikTok | 3–5/week (Reels re-exported) |
| YouTube Shorts | 2–4/week (same as TikTok/Reels) |
