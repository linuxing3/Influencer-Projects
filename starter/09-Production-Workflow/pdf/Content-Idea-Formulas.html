Content Idea Formulas

Reusable Patterns for Endless Posts

When the idea bank runs low, plug your niche into these formulas. Each formula works for feed, carousel, or Reel with small changes.

1 — Before / After

Before: [pain, mess, confusion, old way]
After: [calm, clarity, result, new way]
Show: side-by-side images or a short transition Reel
Caption: one sentence on what changed

Examples: messy desk → clean desk · tourist chaos → local café calm · random outfits → capsule formula

2 — List Post

Number: [3 / 5 / 7 / 10]
Topic: [things / places / tips / mistakes / tools]
Audience: [who this is for]
Promise: [what they get by the end]

Carousel structure: cover with number → one item per slide → final CTA (save / comment / link in bio)

3 — Story Beat

Hook: [odd detail or emotion]
Scene: [where / when]
Turn: [what surprised you]
Lesson: [one takeaway]
Close: [soft CTA or question]

Works as a long caption under one strong photo, or 4–6 carousel slides.

4 — Myth vs Reality

Myth: “[common belief in niche]”
Reality: “[what is actually true]”
Proof: [example, mini-story, or tip]

Great for Reels: text on screen, 10–20 seconds.

5 — Day in the Life

Morning: [ritual]
Midday: [work or explore moment]
Evening: [wind-down or highlight]
Through-line: [mood or theme of the day]

Keep it aesthetic, not a full diary. Three to five scenes max.

6 — Place Reveal

Tease: partial view or detail
Reveal: full location beauty shot
Value: one tip for visiting or recreating the vibe
Tag: [city / vibe / type of place]

7 — Outfit Formula

Base: [core piece]
Layer: [jacket / jewelry / bag]
Shoes: [type]
Palette: [2–3 colors]
Occasion: [when to wear it]

Carousel: full look → details → formula summary slide.

8 — Problem → Framework

Problem: [audience struggle]
Framework name: [simple 2–4 word name]
Steps: 1 · 2 · 3 (max 5)
Next action: [one thing to do today]

Ideal for educational carousels and authority building.

9 — Soft Sell

Value first: [tip or scene that helps without buying]
Bridge: “this is what I use / made / recommend for…”
Offer: [product / affiliate / freebie]
CTA: link in bio / comment KEYWORD / save for later
Disclosure: #ad or paid partnership when required

Use on promo calendar slots only (~20% of posts).

10 — Engagement Engine

Prompt type: this-or-that · finish the sentence · poll · unpopular opinion
Low friction: answerable in 1–3 words
Follow-up: reply to comments with a related tip or Story

11 — Hook Stack (for Reels)

Combine with 02-Prompts/Bonus-30-YouTube-Hooks.md.

Hook (0–2s): [pattern interrupt or bold claim]
Body (3–12s): [demo / list / reveal]
Payoff (last 3s): [result or save CTA]

12 — Series Formula

Series title: [name]
Episode length: [days or weeks]
Episode structure: same format every time
Cliffhanger: what episode N+1 will cover

Examples: 7 days of [city] · 5 outfits · 30 days of prompts
